## 2. Purpose

The Founder has created, commissioned, directed and/or controlled material used for the HOY platform and product family. The parties want the Company to hold a clean, transferable chain of title for all Founder-controlled HOY IP, while preserving third-party/open-source/data-source exclusions.

## 3. Assigned Assets

Subject to Schedule A and the exclusions below, **Assigned Assets** include all rights held by the Founder in the following HOY-created materials existing up to the Effective Date:

1. source code, scripts, tests, build/release files, service-worker code, database migrations and Edge Functions;
2. database schemas, original taxonomies, HOY-created data models, data dictionaries, provenance/trust/freshness models and matching logic;
3. HOY Core, Gastro, Lifestyle and Works product architecture and specifications;
4. HOY NOW, Trust/Verification, Accessibility, Intelligence/Analytics and Region Operating System specifications and original implementation material;
5. original UX/UI layouts, design-system material, icons/graphics/logos created by or validly assigned to the Founder;
6. original documentation, SOPs, research methods, quality policies, commercial models and Investor/Buyer Ready materials;
7. original editorial copy and translations to the extent the Founder owns the necessary rights;
8. domain names, social handles, trade-name/mark rights, trademark applications/registrations and design rights personally held by the Founder and expressly listed in Schedule A;
9. patentable inventions, patent/application rights and other transferable technical rights personally held by the Founder and expressly listed in Schedule A;
10. rights already validly assigned to the Founder by employees, contractors or other contributors, but only to the extent of that prior assignment.

## 4. Exclusive copyright usage rights

For each Assigned Asset protected by copyright or related rights, the Founder grants the Company, to the fullest extent legally permissible, **exclusive**, worldwide, transferable and sublicensable rights of use for the full legal term of protection, including in particular the right to:

- reproduce, store, host, cache, archive and back up;
- distribute and otherwise place copies into circulation;
- make available to the public, communicate publicly and transmit electronically;
- operate as websites, PWAs, native/hybrid apps, SaaS, APIs, dashboards, databases, data products and internal tooling;
- edit, modify, translate, localise, combine, split, refactor, port, migrate and create derivative/adapted versions;
- integrate into other software/products and permit customers, contractors, affiliates, acquirers and service providers to use it as needed for the business;
- sublicense and transfer the rights, including in financing, restructuring, asset sale, share sale/change-of-control and other M&A transactions.

The Company may exercise these rights under the HOY name or another name and may discontinue, replace or combine individual components.

## 5. Unknown future types of use

To the extent rights for currently unknown types of use can validly be granted under applicable law, the Founder agrees to grant them to the Company in the legally required form and on the statutory conditions. The final signed version must specifically address the German-law form and statutory remuneration/revocation rules applicable to unknown types of use; this clause is not intended to waive rights that cannot legally be waived in advance.

## 6. Modifications, attribution and moral rights

To the extent legally permissible, the Founder consents to modifications, adaptations, translations, combinations, technical changes and product redesigns required for the Company's business and authorises the Company to decide whether and how the Founder is credited.

Nothing in this agreement purports to transfer or waive an inalienable moral right. Instead, the Founder agrees not to object to ordinary commercial modifications and non-attribution to the extent legally effective, subject to mandatory law.

## 7. Assignment of other transferable rights

To the extent owned by the Founder and listed in Schedule A, the Founder assigns to the Company all transferable rights, title and interest in:

- trademark, trade-name and other sign rights;
- domain names and related registrar rights;
- registered/unregistered design rights;
- database-maker/sui-generis database rights arising from the Founder's qualifying investment, if any;
- patent rights, patent applications and rights to apply for patents;
- claims against third parties arising from infringement occurring after the Effective Date, to the extent legally assignable;
- contractual IP rights already validly acquired by the Founder, subject to their transfer restrictions.

Any registry/registrar transfer requiring a separate form or filing will be completed under Section 11 (Further Assurances).

## 8. Data and database boundary

The assignment includes HOY-created database structure, selection/organisation, original taxonomies, provenance metadata, verification history, HOY-created measurements, first-party workflow data and other rights that the Founder actually owns.

It **does not** claim ownership of a third party's facts, menu/source content, photographs, marketplace/directory material, licensed/open data beyond its licence, operator-owned media, or personal data merely because HOY stores or processes it. Such material remains governed by the IR-02A Data Rights & Provenance Register and the applicable contracts/licences/privacy law.

## 9. Third-party and open-source materials

The assignment excludes third-party/open-source components except for the rights validly held by the Founder under their licences. Known dependencies and licence obligations are maintained in the HOY OSS/Dependency Register.

The Company takes the Assigned Assets subject to the documented third-party licences and source-rights restrictions rather than receiving a false representation that all repository content is proprietary.

## 10. Founder representations

Subject to Schedule B (Exceptions), the Founder represents to the best of the Founder's knowledge on the Effective Date that:

1. the Founder has not knowingly assigned the same exclusive rights in the Assigned Assets to another party;
2. the Founder has not knowingly granted an undisclosed security interest, licence or encumbrance inconsistent with this assignment;
3. known third-party components/data/media are not intentionally represented as Founder-owned proprietary IP;
4. any known human contributor whose work requires a separate assignment will be listed in the Contributor Register rather than silently treated as Founder-owned;
5. the Founder will disclose any prior employment/contract obligation that may reasonably claim rights in an Assigned Asset;
6. AI-assisted creation is disclosed as such where material and is not represented as automatically giving the Company exclusive copyright protection in machine-generated material.

These representations must be confirmed by the Founder before signature; the present draft does not itself certify facts not yet independently audited.
