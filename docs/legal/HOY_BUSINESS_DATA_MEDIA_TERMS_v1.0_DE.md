## 1. Purpose and scope

These Business Data & Media Terms (the **“Business Terms”**) govern the relationship between HOY and a business, venue, service provider or other commercial operator (the **“Business”**) that claims, manages, confirms, uploads or otherwise supplies business-related information or content to HOY.

They are designed to cover, where applicable:

- business profile data;
- addresses, contact details and opening hours;
- services, facilities and accessibility characteristics;
- menu, product and price information;
- specials, events and live/temporary status information;
- photographs, videos, logos and other media;
- corrections and confirmations;
- operator-supplied URLs and source references;
- Business-confirmed structured attributes;
- operational content supplied through HOY tools.

These Business Terms do **not** by themselves govern end-user/customer privacy, payment-provider terms, consumer contracts or any separate data-processing agreement that may be required under Article 28 GDPR.

## 2. Contracting party and authority

The person accepting these Business Terms represents that they:

1. are at least 18 years old and legally capable of contracting;
2. are the owner, authorized manager, employee, representative or otherwise duly authorized person of the Business;
3. have authority to bind the Business for the permissions and obligations described in these Business Terms; and
4. will notify HOY if that authority ceases.

HOY may require additional verification before activating operator rights or marking information as **Business Confirmed**.

Acceptance of these Business Terms alone does not prove ownership of a venue or business. Operator verification and rights confirmation are separate controls.

## 3. Definitions

### 3.1 Business Content

**Business Content** means content or materials supplied, uploaded, selected, approved or expressly licensed by the Business, including text, menus, logos, images, videos, event materials and similar protectable content.

### 3.2 Business Facts

**Business Facts** means factual or operational information about the Business, such as opening hours, address, services, accessibility characteristics, prices, current availability and similar information.

### 3.3 Business Confirmed Data

**Business Confirmed Data** means a Business Fact that HOY records as having been confirmed by an authenticated and authorized Business representative at a recorded date/time.

“Business Confirmed” describes the source and verification state. It does not mean HOY guarantees that the information will remain correct indefinitely.

### 3.4 HOY Data Layer

**HOY Data Layer** means HOY-created structures and information including taxonomies, schemas, normalized fields, classifications, timestamps, provenance records, verification states, freshness states, audit history, matching structures, confidence logic, operational metadata and similar HOY-created system data.

### 3.5 Derived and Aggregated Data

**Derived Data** means new data or insights created by HOY from lawfully usable inputs through normalization, comparison, scoring, aggregation, statistical processing or product operation.

**Aggregated Data** means data combined so that it is not intended to disclose raw Business Content or identify an individual person, subject always to applicable law.

## 4. Business retains ownership of its content

Except for rights expressly granted to HOY under these Business Terms, the Business retains its rights in Business Content that it owns.

HOY does **not** claim that accepting these Business Terms transfers ownership of the Business's logo, photographs, marketing copy, original menu design or other Business Content to HOY.

Nothing in these Business Terms permits HOY to represent that third-party material belongs to HOY merely because it was submitted or referenced by a Business.

## 5. Licence granted to HOY for Business Content

To the extent Business Content is protected by copyright, database rights, trademark rights or similar rights and the Business has authority to grant the relevant permission, the Business grants HOY a **non-exclusive, royalty-free licence** for the purposes described below.

### 5.1 Territory

The licence applies **worldwide**, including all territories in which HOY operates, markets or makes its services accessible.

### 5.2 Duration

The licence applies for the duration of the Business's relationship with HOY and, after termination, for the limited periods reasonably required for:

- orderly removal from active public display;
- backups and disaster recovery;
- legal/compliance records;
- dispute evidence;
- audit and provenance history.

Public promotional use of Business Content should cease within a reasonable operational period after effective termination, unless another legal basis or separate agreement applies.

### 5.3 Permitted modes of use

The Business authorizes HOY to the extent necessary for HOY services to:

- store and host Business Content;
- reproduce it technically;
- display and communicate it to the public through HOY products;
- resize, crop, compress and technically optimize media;
- format and normalize text and structured data;
- translate and localize Business-supplied text while preserving its commercial meaning;
- create platform-compatible representations of menus, offers, events and similar materials;
- distribute or transmit Business Content through HOY's technical service providers, CDNs, hosting providers and other subcontractors as required to provide the service;
- use Business Content to identify and promote the Business within HOY and reasonable HOY marketing relating to the Business or HOY marketplace/platform.

### 5.4 Sublicensing

HOY may grant limited sublicences to hosting, infrastructure, localization, analytics, content-delivery, development and other service providers only as necessary to operate, secure, develop or support HOY.

Such sublicensing does not authorize those providers to use Business Content for unrelated independent purposes.

### 5.5 Transfer / change of control

The Business agrees that the rights granted to HOY under these Business Terms may be transferred or continued in connection with:

- a merger;
- corporate restructuring;
- sale of shares;
- sale of substantially all relevant assets;
- acquisition of HOY or a relevant HOY business unit;
- other bona fide change-of-control transaction,

provided the successor continues to use the rights for the HOY service/business or a substantially similar successor service and remains bound by the applicable contractual/data-protection obligations.

This clause is intended to make legitimate HOY business relationships transferable in an investment or company sale; it does not create ownership of content that the Business did not have authority to license.

## 6. What the licence does not permit by default

Unless separately agreed, HOY will not treat these Business Terms as permission to:

- sell the Business's raw photographs, logo or marketing copy as a standalone content library;
- license the Business's raw menu artwork or original media to unrelated third parties for unrelated purposes;
- imply endorsement of a third party by the Business;
- remove legally required attribution or rights-management information;
- knowingly use Business Content outside the purposes described in these Business Terms.

This limitation does not restrict HOY's rights in HOY-created Data Layer elements, Derived Data or Aggregated Data as described below.

## 7. Business Facts and confirmations

The Business authorizes HOY to record, structure, normalize, display, update and retain Business Facts supplied or confirmed by the Business.

HOY may attach to a Business Fact:

- confirmation timestamp;
- confirming operator/user identifier;
- source type;
- freshness state;
- verification level;
- audit/history information;
- change history;
- related HOY-created classifications or normalized values.

A Business may correct its Business Facts through the available HOY process.

HOY may preserve historical versions where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, audit, dispute handling, security, compliance, data-quality measurement or system history, even if the current public value changes.

## 8. Accuracy and freshness

The Business is responsible for taking reasonable care that Business-supplied or Business-confirmed information is accurate and not misleading at the time of confirmation.

The Business should update or correct information that materially changes, including where applicable:

- permanent closure or relocation;
- material opening-hours changes;
- temporary closures;
- menu/pricing changes where supplied to HOY;
- accessibility characteristics;
- active services;
- live availability/status;
- events and specials.

HOY may expire, downgrade, hide or mark information as requiring confirmation if it becomes stale, disputed or inconsistent with reliable evidence.

## 9. Accessibility and safety-sensitive facts

Accessibility information is recorded as specific attributes rather than a universal promise that a venue is “accessible”.

When a Business confirms accessibility information, it confirms the specific facts submitted to HOY, such as entrance, seating, toilet, parking or measured characteristics.

Where a measurement is supplied (for example, door width or number of steps), the Business confirms that the measurement was actually obtained and is not knowingly fabricated or guessed.

HOY may distinguish Business Confirmed information from HOY Verified, Community Confirmed and External/Unverified information.

## 10. Media rights and operator approval

For each photograph, video, logo, menu image or other media asset submitted or approved for publication, the Business represents that it has sufficient rights or permissions for the intended HOY use.

The Business must not upload or approve media where it knows it lacks the required rights.

Where a Business approves media sourced from its own website or official account, that approval means the Business authorizes HOY's use under Section 5 to the extent the Business has the legal authority to do so.

If a third party owns the relevant rights, the Business must obtain any necessary permission or tell HOY that separate licensing is required.

HOY may keep unapproved candidate media private and unpublished until rights/approval is established.

## 11. Menu and product information

Where a Business submits or confirms menu information, the Business authorizes HOY to structure and display the factual menu information and, where applicable, translate/localize the text for HOY users.

The Business retains rights in original menu artwork, branding, photographs and protectable copy that it owns.

HOY retains rights in HOY-created normalization, category mapping, structured fields, translations created by or for HOY to the extent legally protectable, provenance data and platform presentation logic, subject to underlying third-party rights.

Prices and availability may change. HOY may display freshness/last-confirmed information and may stop presenting stale menu data as current.

---

## 12. Offers, specials, events and HOY NOW / live status

When a Business publishes or confirms an offer, event, opening status, temporary availability or similar live information, it authorizes HOY to display and distribute that information for the applicable period.

The Business is responsible for the commercial accuracy of its own published offer terms and must not submit unlawful, misleading or deceptive promotions.

HOY may automatically expire time-sensitive information after its stated validity period or according to product freshness rules.

## 13. HOY-created data, metadata and derived intelligence

The Business acknowledges that HOY independently creates and controls the HOY Data Layer, including:

- data models and schemas;
- category/taxonomy structures;
- provenance metadata;
- verification and trust states;
- freshness calculations;
- change and confirmation history;
- matching logic;
- operational logs;
- platform performance data;
- non-personal product analytics;
- lawful Derived Data and Aggregated Data.

Subject to applicable law and third-party rights, HOY may use Derived Data and Aggregated Data to:

- operate and improve HOY;
- measure market/product performance;
- build recommendations and matching;
- identify regional demand and supply patterns;
- develop analytics products;
- perform internal research;
- prepare aggregated commercial insights.

These Business Terms do **not** authorize disclosure or sale of personal data contrary to data-protection law and do not authorize HOY to resell raw Business Content as a standalone content product unless separately agreed.

## 14. No transfer of personal-data “ownership”

Personal data is not treated as an owned commodity under these Business Terms.

Where Business contact information or user information constitutes personal data, HOY will process it under the applicable privacy notice and a valid legal basis.

The parties' privacy roles depend on the processing activity.

If HOY processes personal data solely on documented instructions and on behalf of the Business as a processor, the parties will enter into a separate data-processing agreement meeting the requirements of Article 28 GDPR before that processing requires it.

Acceptance of these Business Terms is not itself an Article 28 data-processing agreement.

## 16. HOY rights-control and takedown process

HOY may suspend, hide or remove Business Content or data where HOY reasonably believes that:

- rights are unclear;
- a third party raises a credible rights claim;
- information is materially inaccurate;
- the Business's authority is disputed;
- content creates legal, security or safety risk;
- continued display would breach law or platform policy.

The Business may notify HOY of corrections, rights restrictions or removal requests using the contact/process specified in the final activated version.

HOY may retain a non-public audit record of the dispute/removal where lawfully necessary.

## 19. Termination and effect on rights

Either party's termination rights will be defined by the relevant commercial plan/order form and applicable law.

After effective termination:

- HOY will stop new active operator publishing access;
- public Business Content will be removed or disabled within a reasonable operational period where the continuing licence no longer applies;
- HOY may retain transaction, audit, security, rights and provenance records where legally justified;
- HOY may retain lawfully created Aggregated Data and Derived Data that does not reproduce protected raw Business Content or violate applicable privacy obligations;
- historical confirmation records may remain as non-public evidence that a fact was confirmed at a particular time.

Termination does not retroactively invalidate uses that were lawful while the agreement was in force.

# Legal implementation notes — not part of customer-facing text after finalization

- Spanish electronic-contract rules recognize electronic contracts and require appropriate pre-contract information and the ability to store/reproduce general conditions; acceptance evidence/confirmation should be retained.
- Spanish copyright law requires exploitation rights/modes, duration and territorial scope to be sufficiently specified; this draft therefore expressly states permitted uses, worldwide territory, duration and transfer/change-of-control mechanics rather than using a vague blanket “all rights” clause.
- GDPR personal-data processing requires its own legal basis and purpose limitation; Article 28 processor relationships require a separate written/electronic data-processing contract where HOY truly acts as processor for the Business.
- Database/content rights remain separate from ownership of individual facts; these Terms intentionally license Business Content while preserving HOY's separately created data/provenance layer.
