## Assignment schedule — minimum asset categories

A formal Chain-of-Title instrument should explicitly schedule, where legally applicable:

- source code, migrations, Edge Functions and scripts;
- database schemas and HOY-created taxonomies;
- original UX/UI designs and original brand assets;
- HOY-created documentation, standards, research methodologies and data models;
- original translations/editorial content to the extent rights are transferable;
- rights in contractor/employee deliverables already assigned to the founder;
- domain/trademark/application rights where held personally;
- relevant repositories, build/release artifacts and operational documentation.

## Exclusions that must remain exclusions

The assignment schedule must **not** imply ownership of:

- third-party/open-source libraries;
- third-party business facts or menu/source content merely because HOY stores them;
- operator media not assigned/licensed to HOY;
- external directory/marketplace content;
- personal data as an IP asset;
- public/open data beyond the applicable licence rights.

## Contributor evidence protocol

For each repository, the final DD file must retain:

1. contributor identity/login;
2. contribution period;
3. relationship to HOY/founder (founder, employee, contractor, external contributor, bot/tool);
4. whether copyright/usage rights arise automatically or require assignment under the applicable relationship/jurisdiction;
5. signed agreement/evidence reference if required;
6. known exceptions or third-party copied/adapted material;
7. status: `CLEAR`, `ASSIGNMENT_REQUIRED`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `EXCLUDED_THIRD_PARTY`.
