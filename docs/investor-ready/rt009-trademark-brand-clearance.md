## 1. Current brand fact pattern

Current production assets use **HOY** as the dominant consumer mark. The current app icon renders `HOY` prominently and adds the geographic descriptor `LA MANGA`.

Working platform family:
- HOY
- HOY Gastro
- HOY Lifestyle
- HOY Works
- potential later modules incl. HOY Safe

Consumer proposition intentionally leans into the temporal meaning: current/local intent, i.e. “today / now”.

## 2. Core red-team conclusion

`HOY` means **“today” in Spanish**. That creates a material absolute-ground/distinctiveness risk for services whose value proposition is explicitly “what can I do/eat/book today/now?”. Under EU/Spanish trademark principles, descriptive or non-distinctive signs can be refused for the relevant goods/services. Because Spanish is an EU language, an EU mark cannot be treated as safe merely because the term may be distinctive in other countries.

This does **not** mean HOY must be abandoned. It means:
1. do not model a strong monopoly in the word `HOY` as a proven asset;
2. do not file blindly;
3. do not let a future financing/exit depend on one weak word mark;
4. preserve a rebrand/house-mark path before large sunk brand cost.

## 3. Relative-right / collision risk

There is a known Spanish market-use signal for `HOY` in media/publishing (including the well-known Spanish `HOY` newspaper/Vocento context). Exact active registrations, owners, goods/services, seniority, status and territorial scope must be verified in TMview/EUIPO/OEPM/WIPO before any filing or investor claim.

**Important:** until an exact registry record is attached, HOY does not state that any specific third party owns a particular live registration. Market use is a conflict signal, not a registry conclusion.

Relative-right search must also cover:
- identical `HOY` marks;
- `HOY` as dominant element in composite marks;
- phonetic/visual/conceptual near matches;
- `HOY GASTRO`, `HOY LIFESTYLE`, `HOY WORKS`, `HOY SAFE`;
- `MIRA HOY` and other intended slogans;
- live Spanish national marks, EUTMs and international registrations designating ES/EU;
- relevant reputed marks even outside identical Nice classes.

## 5. Binding brand architecture

### Preferred architecture

**Invented House Mark / Parent Brand**
- owns HOY IP, software, data architecture, Region OS, domains and registrations;
- should be a coined/distinctive term with low semantic descriptiveness;
- can remain mostly corporate/investor-facing initially.

**HOY — consumer product brand**
- can continue if clearance is acceptable;
- treated as potentially weaker/narrower than the house mark;
- may be protected through word and/or figurative filings depending counsel clearance.

**HOY + vertical descriptors**
- product/navigation architecture, not assumed strong standalone marks;
- do not overpay for broad filings unless commercial use and clearance justify them.

This architecture preserves consumer brand equity while removing “HOY word mark or rebrand” as a single point of failure.

## 6. Filing-scope working model

Do **not** file until the exact goods/services specification is counsel-reviewed and the live-search matrix is attached.

Likely core classes based on the current operating model:
- **Class 9** — downloadable software/mobile application where actually offered;
- **Class 35** — business information, advertising/promotion, marketplace/intermediation-related services as actually supplied;
- **Class 42** — SaaS/software platform/technology services;
- **Class 43** — restaurant information/reservation-related services only to the extent HOY actually provides them.

Conditional / later:
- **Class 41** — event/entertainment information or booking only if HOY supplies a registrable service in that field;
- **Class 39** — travel/transport information/arrangement only if material to the actual service;
- other classes only after use-case and counsel review.

Explicit guardrails:
- **Do not file Class 37 merely because HOY Works lists tradespeople.** HOY should claim construction/repair services only if HOY itself supplies those services.
- do not claim payment/financial, security or travel services HOY does not actually perform;
- avoid broad speculative lists that increase cost, conflict surface and non-use exposure.

## 7. Figurative mark strategy

The current icon has a distinctive dark-blue/cream/orange treatment and stylised `HOY` word rendering. A figurative filing may be a useful layer if cleared.

But:
- a figurative registration protects the mark as filed, not a guaranteed broad monopoly over the ordinary word `HOY`;
- a future material logo redesign may require a new filing/use analysis;
- exact source files, design provenance and creator rights belong in RT-004/006;
- no ® symbol until an applicable registration actually exists.

## 8. Brand-use governance from today

Until RT-009 closes:
- use `HOY` consistently as a brand (capitalised/stylised) where possible;
- do not state “registered trademark”, “protected throughout the EU” or similar;
- do not use ®;
- ™ may be used only as an unregistered-trademark indicator where commercially appropriate and counsel does not object;
- archive dated first-use evidence by territory/service;
- archive icon/logo source files and changes;
- archive domain/handle registrations under RT-005;
- avoid granting third parties unrestricted ownership in HOY branding;
- business/media terms must make clear who owns HOY marks and brand assets.

## 10. Decision tree

### GREEN
Professional search finds no blocking prior right and counsel considers absolute-ground risk acceptable.
- file the selected HOY layer(s) with exact class scope;
- file/clear invented house mark independently;
- record filing/registration in Parent asset schedule.

### AMBER
HOY may be registrable only figuratively/narrowly, or prior rights can coexist with restrictions.
- keep HOY consumer-facing if commercially sensible;
- house mark becomes mandatory;
- narrow classes/specification;
- no investor claim of broad HOY exclusivity;
- obtain written coexistence/risk analysis only if needed.

### RED
Blocking prior right, strong opposition/infringement risk, or unacceptable absolute-ground weakness.
- do not sink further capital into exclusive HOY brand claims;
- activate cleared invented master/product mark;
- preserve HOY only as descriptive campaign language if counsel says safe, or retire it.
