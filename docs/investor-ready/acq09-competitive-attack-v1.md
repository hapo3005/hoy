# HOY ACQ-09 — Competitive Attack v1.0

**Status:** ACTIVE_INTERNAL_COMPETITIVE_STRATEGY  
**Evidence snapshot:** 2026-08-21  
**Scope:** Full defined Mar Menor Region; Gastro is the first measurable proof vertical, not the platform boundary.

## Why this exists

The discovery of active local competitors changes HOY's risk picture in a useful way. We no longer need to treat local digital discovery as an untested category assumption: current public evidence shows active destination editorial, restaurant discovery, local-business directory mechanics and menu/price discovery around La Manga and the Murcia region.

That is **market-category evidence, not proof that HOY has demand, product-market fit, market share or a competitive advantage**.

The strategic response is therefore not to copy a directory. It is to use the mechanisms competitors have validated as table stakes, then make HOY measurably stronger where a static directory is structurally weak: current context, trusted freshness, structured multilingual decision data, accessibility matching, actionability, merchant confirmation, economic outcomes and cross-vertical/region repeatability.

## Current external evidence

### Viviendo La Manga

Observed public evidence on 2026-08-21:

- `https://viviendolamanga.es/blog/` actively publishes La Manga/Cabo de Palos guides across restaurants, family plans, activities, beaches and destination topics. A kitesurf guide was published on 2026-08-17.
- Cadena SER reported on 2026-07-23 that Viviendo La Manga creator Marioly Vivancos participated in a La Manga local radio event and shared recommendations about plans and lesser-known places.

What this supports: local editorial discovery, destination-specific content, restaurant/activity guides and observable local media presence.

What it **does not** support: user count, revenue, market share, SEO traffic or commercial conversion.

### TuLocalidad / El Paladar de la Huerta

Observed public evidence on 2026-08-21:

- TuLocalidad presents itself as a digital showcase connecting local businesses with nearby residents.
- El Paladar de la Huerta publishes search-oriented Murcia restaurant guides and routes readers toward TuLocalidad for geo-located menus and current prices. Its 2026 hamburger guide self-reports analysis of 312 menus; that quantitative claim is not treated by HOY as independently audited evidence.

What this supports: local-business directory mechanics, editorial-to-directory funneling, menu/price discovery and geo-local business discovery.

What it **does not** support: independently audited scale, users, revenue, market share, SEO traffic or conversion.

## Positioning rule

A directory primarily answers:

> **What exists here?**

HOY must answer:

> **What fits me now, why can I trust it, and what can I do next?**

Coverage matters, but coverage alone is not a moat. HOY must not win by becoming a larger generic directory.

## Differentiation pillars

1. **NOW context and freshness** — current decision state instead of static listings.
2. **Trust and field-level verification** — verified/confirmed/unknown remain explicit.
3. **Native structured multilingual menus** — uniform HOY presentation in DE/ES/EN, not source dumps.
4. **Accessibility requirement matching** — MUST/PREFER/IGNORE and unknown-as-confirmation-required.
5. **Personalized decision support** — context before browsing volume.
6. **High-intent action handoffs** — route, call, reservation, services and other useful next actions.
7. **Merchant first-party confirmation** — direct business truth without conflating facts with rights clearance.
8. **Privacy-safe measurable merchant value** — intent and outcomes kept semantically separate.
9. **Cross-vertical local intent graph** — shared Gastro/Lifestyle/Works primitives.
10. **Region replication without core forks** — platform value instead of one-off local craftsmanship.

## Competitive Attack Tracks

### CAT-01 — Coverage and discoverability parity — P0

Measure Region-1 business/category/area coverage against an explicit versioned baseline. Build canonical indexable area/category/use-case surfaces only where they answer real user intent. Structured metadata and canonical URLs are allowed; doorway pages, thin SEO pages and traffic-for-traffic's-sake are not.

**Proof:** coverage baseline; quality-checked intent surfaces; later, qualified search sessions tied to downstream behavior.

### CAT-02 — Freshness and HOY NOW superiority — P0

Unknown, stale and unverified states remain fail-closed. Current opening/service/offer/event/live-status facts only surface under their verification rules. Measure critical-field freshness SLA and stale rates.

**Proof:** freshness SLA, stale rate by field, operator-confirmed current-state coverage.

### CAT-03 — Menu decision advantage — P0

Complete HOY-native structured menus in DE, ES and EN under the strict current-language guest contract. Preserve source truth, integrity and rights boundaries. Structured dishes may support price/dietary/decision use cases only when quality permits.

**Proof:** guest-ready trilingual coverage; menu integrity rate; later menu-to-high-intent behavior.

### CAT-04 — Decision UX instead of directory UX — P0

Help the user choose rather than browse. Prioritize evidence-backed context such as open-now state, time, distance, budget, dietary needs, family context and accessibility requirements. Do not hide uncertainty behind a universal opaque score.

**Proof:** decision-surface usage; qualified high-intent rate; repeat usage from decision surfaces.

### CAT-05 — Trust and first-party confirmation — P0

Expand factual operator confirmation only through verified merchant paths. Keep factual confirmation separate from rights-backed receipts and commercial clearance.

**Proof:** merchant factual-confirmation coverage; freshness within SLA; rights-backed coverage reported separately.

### CAT-06 — Actionability and merchant outcome — P0

Maintain route, website, call, reservation and service-handoff events under ACQ-04 semantics. A click/handoff is never a confirmed transaction. Merchant-acknowledged leads and confirmed outcomes stay separate from intent.

**Proof:** qualified high-intent sessions; merchant-acknowledged leads; merchant-confirmed transaction/value evidence.

### CAT-07 — Editorial authority without generic SEO content — P1

Compete with strong local editorial discovery using region-specific useful content, not generic AI-looking volume. Editorial pages must connect to live structured HOY data rather than duplicate facts that drift independently.

**Proof:** editorial-to-decision-surface rate; editorial-assisted high intent; duplicate-fact drift rate.

### CAT-08 — Cross-vertical local intent advantage — P1

Reuse shared location, identity, trust, intent and action primitives across Gastro, Lifestyle, Works and future verticals. A vertical-specific core fork is not an acceptable shortcut.

**Proof:** cross-vertical sessions; shared-core reuse; second-vertical launch cost/hours.

### CAT-09 — Paid merchant proof — P1

Turn differentiation into economic evidence. ACQ-03 remains binding: only reconciled settled external payment is Paying Business proof. One-off Highlight revenue is not recurring MRR.

**Proof:** external paying businesses; recurring MRR excluding Highlights; renewal; 90-day paid retention.

### CAT-10 — Competitive evidence refresh — P1

Re-verify public competitor surfaces before material positioning or investor claims. Version observations by date/source and keep unknowns explicit.

**Proof:** dated refreshes; zero unsupported claims; strategy changes traceable to evidence.

## Anti-theater rules

- Traffic is not value without qualified downstream behavior.
- Competitor visibility does not reveal popularity, market share or revenue.
- A larger directory is not the strategy.
