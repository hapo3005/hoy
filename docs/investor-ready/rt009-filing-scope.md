# RT-009 — Filing Scope / Brand Architecture Draft

Internal working specification only. Final wording must be drafted/approved by qualified EU/Spanish trademark counsel against the Nice Classification version current at filing.

## 1. Core filing layers

### Layer A — Invented house/master word mark
**Priority: highest for exit resilience.**

Purpose:
- Parent/company identity;
- ownership layer for software, data, Region OS and central contracts;
- expandable across future verticals/regions;
- independent from the Spanish descriptive meaning of HOY.

Must be independently cleared before selection. Do not publicly launch a candidate before clearance if avoidable.

### Layer B — HOY word mark
**Priority: commercially desirable, legally uncertain.**

Attempt only if live search + counsel conclude acceptable. Do not count it in valuation as a strong exclusive right before filing/registration and scope analysis.

### Layer C — HOY figurative/logo mark
**Priority: useful secondary layer.**

Use the final intended design. Preserve exact vector, colour references, creator/AI/tool provenance and dated first-use evidence.

### Layer D — vertical names
- HOY Gastro
- HOY Lifestyle
- HOY Works
- later HOY Safe

Treat initially as product descriptors/sub-brands. File selectively only where distinctiveness, use and value justify the cost/conflict surface.

## 3. Draft goods/services principles

The final specification should:
- use pre-approved Nice terms where commercially accurate;
- be narrow enough to reduce conflict and non-use exposure;
- cover the platform business, not every underlying business category listed in HOY;
- distinguish downloadable software (class 9) from SaaS (class 42);
- distinguish advertising/business marketplace functions (class 35) from underlying provider services;
- avoid claiming hotels, restaurants, plumbers, builders, transport or activities as HOY’s own service unless the commercial model changes.

## 4. Territory strategy

Working priority:
1. Spain/EU clearance first because Region #001 and Spanish meaning drive the highest risk.
2. Decide EUTM vs Spanish national filing based on clearance, absolute-ground risk, expansion plan and counsel strategy.
3. Later international expansion via Madrid/national filings only after the mark architecture is stable.

Do not assume an EUTM is always optimal: a Spanish-language absolute-ground issue can affect EU-wide registration strategy.

## 5. Use evidence / non-use resilience

Maintain evidence by mark, class, territory and service:
- dated product screenshots;
- app/web release history;
- invoices/paid subscriptions when live;
- advertising and campaign records;
- contracts/business terms showing the mark;
- region launch materials;
- user/operator metrics tied to branded services;
- logo/source-file history.

## 6. Deck / investor claim guardrails

Until registered and legally reviewed:
- say `HOY brand / brand architecture`;
- do not say `EU-protected trademark`, `registered mark`, `exclusive rights across Europe`;
- if relevant in DD: `Trademark clearance is a P0 legal gate; current architecture preserves an invented-house-mark fallback.`

## 7. External-action freeze

No trademark application, opposition approach, coexistence request or rights-holder contact is authorised by this draft. Filing follows only after Parent/entity/IP/tax mechanics and qualified clearance are approved.
