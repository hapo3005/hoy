# HOY RT-008 — Layered Privacy Notice Draft

Status: **DRAFT / NOT FOR PUBLICATION** until controller identity, contact details, final vendors, legal bases, retention and national-law review are completed. Spanish/English localization must follow the legally reviewed master.

## Layer 1 — short notice

### Who is responsible?
HOY’s final legal controller entity and privacy contact will be inserted after RT-003 formation/validation.

### What does HOY process?
Depending on how you use HOY:
- technical data needed to deliver the service;
- optional analytics identifiers and usage events **only after analytics opt-in**;
- account and contact data if you create/manage a business/provider account;
- information you submit in a business claim, menu/profile update or Works request;
- Works photos if you voluntarily upload them for a request;
- communications and security/audit information.

### Why?
To provide the requested HOY service, manage accounts/business relationships, keep the service secure, process Works requests and—only with the required permission—measure product usage.

### Does HOY sell personal data?
No. HOY’s planned data/BI business is based on rights-cleared aggregated/anonymised insights, not the sale of identifiable user or contact data.

### Optional analytics
Analytics is optional. If you do not opt in, HOY’s core service remains available and no HOY analytics UUID/session identifier is created for production analytics. You can withdraw the choice through the final privacy/consent control once implemented.

### Who receives data?
Only recipients necessary for the selected feature and documented in the final processor/recipient register, such as HOY’s backend provider, and—for a Works request—the assigned provider where necessary to perform the requested service. The final notice will list active recipient categories and international-transfer safeguards.

### Your rights
Subject to the GDPR and applicable exceptions, you may request access, rectification, deletion, restriction, portability or object to certain processing and may complain to the competent data protection authority. The final controller privacy contact will be inserted before publication.

## Layer 2 — processing-specific detail

### A. Core service / technical delivery
Only the technical processing necessary to deliver pages, APIs and user-requested actions should occur without optional analytics consent. External browser services such as map tiles/CDNs must be specifically listed if retained in the final architecture.

### B. Analytics
If activated after opt-in, HOY may process a pseudonymous persistent identifier, session identifier, event type/time, selected venue and limited contextual metadata. These identifiers are treated as personal data. Raw production analytics are proposed to be retained for no longer than 90 days; longer-term reporting uses only outputs that pass the approved aggregation/anonymisation standard. Exact duration/withdrawal UI must match the live implementation.

### C. Business/operator accounts and claims
HOY processes account identifiers, business contact details and authority/claim data to authenticate users and allow verified representatives to manage business information. Security/audit events may be recorded.

### D. Business contact research from public sources
Where HOY has obtained professional contact details from public sources rather than directly from the person, the final Article 14 notice must identify the categories of data, purposes/legal basis, source—including whether publicly accessible—retention, recipients and rights. This draft does not itself satisfy the obligation until it is delivered through the legally selected route.

### E. HOY Works
Works may process account/profile data, the work-request description, municipality/location context, assignment/status events and optional photos. Users must be told not to include sensitive information, faces, ID documents, health information or other unnecessary personal data in free text/photos. Information is disclosed only to participants/assigned providers as required by the service and terms.

### F. AI-assisted menu processing
If a business chooses a menu workflow that uses an AI provider, the final notice/business terms must explain the relevant processor and data categories. HOY should minimise personal data in menu inputs. AI output is a draft/review layer; the source/operator remains authoritative for menu facts.

## Retention summary — proposed HOY policy

- raw pseudonymous analytics: max 90 days;
- sales contact records: Article 14 deadline first; then revalidate/delete at 12 months if lawful;
- rejected/abandoned claims/applications: target 6 months;
- app security/audit records: provisional 12 months;
- Works request: provisional active + 6 months after closure;
- Works photos: proposed 30–90 days after closure;
- account deletion: target operational purge/anonymisation within 30 days, subject to legal holds/provider cycles;
- statutory tax/accounting/contract records: separate schedule after legal-entity/jurisdiction sign-off.

These are policy targets and must be reconciled with final legal obligations before publication.
